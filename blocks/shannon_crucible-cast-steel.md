  BE DRAWN. Whitworth's threads were not stronger, they were interchangeable.
  Float glass was not a better surface, it was an inherited one. Crucible

  Three published plates across two lines, and every attempt to wire them

WHAT THE PLATE IS CAREFUL ABOUT, and it is unusually careful. Four claims
  exist to correct a mistake an earlier draft of this plate made, and those
  corrections are kept in the source notes because they are the reason the
  wording is what it is. c-01: slag and the carbon gradient have different
  origins and were conflated. c-02: forging did improve the old steel, and
  saying otherwise contradicts cementation itself. c-04: the crucible was

  removed. c-08 carries the counterintuitive mechanism: the hazard was the

  rational. c-09 records sequence and refuses causation between the failed
  petition and the 1764 furnaces. c-11 uses the museum's own wording.
