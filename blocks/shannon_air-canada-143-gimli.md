WHAT THE PLATE IS. c-07 carries it: the same wrong density factor was used to
  compute the shortfall, to compute the volume to add, and again in the
  recheck at Ottawa, so every figure agreed with every other figure. The
  verification could not detect the error because the error was not in any
  measurement. It sat in the conversion beneath all of them. An all-imperial
  fleet is safe and an all-metric fleet is safe; an airline halfway between
  them is neither.
