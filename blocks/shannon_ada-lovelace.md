PEOPLE
  - name: Ada Lovelace
    role: pioneered
    note: Authored the seven Notes; first published algorithm (Note G, with
      loop and variable indexing); first articulation that the Engine could
      manipulate symbols beyond number, and of the program-versus-data
      distinction.
  - name: Charles Babbage
    role: designed
    note: Designed the unbuilt Analytical Engine; supplied the Bernoulli
      mathematics for Note G; his memoir records the collaboration and that
      the selection of illustrations was entirely hers.
  - name: Luigi Menabrea
    role: pioneered
    note: Published the first account of the Analytical Engine (French,
      1842) from Babbage's only public lecture on it; later prime minister
      of Italy.
